What the movie should show

One muted, looping ~30–40s capture of the live store. Its whole job is to prove one claim — “I built Sanglogium — alone, in production.” Content rules only; no tech.

Forced by the format — not choices

MUTEDNo sound ever reaches the viewer. On-screen text is the soundtrack — every beat needs a caption or it says nothing.
HERO FRAMEThe surrounding page already prints title, runtime, play button. Identity is handled outside the video — a title/logo slate inside it is dead seconds.
LOOPSThe last second hands back to the first. The end slate must be a designed hold, not an appendix.
~35s ÷ 87 wins + slate ≈ 4–5s per beat. Each beat must be readable alone — a viewer can land mid-movie.

What proves “real production store”

Polish doesn’t prove it — hard-to-fake states do. The movie is an evidence ladder, not a tour:

Live catalogReal products, real prices, real data
FilteringFacets actually narrow results — the two callouts
ProductVariants, stock, detail
CartAdds, edits, persists
CheckoutReaches the real payment step — stop before submit

The 7 locked wins are rungs on this ladder. Order already matches it — keep it.

The first frame — your specific question

Skip the slate. No logo, no countdown, no “a film by”. Frame 1 = the storefront, settled, first caption already up. Every measured film in the genre opens on product or a one-line question — none on branding.

Deleted — unnecessary

  • Title/brand slate at the open — the hero frame already does it
  • Tour completeness (every page, every feature) — one flow proves the claim
  • Anything that needs sound to make sense
  • Separate poster decision — the poster is forced: it’s frame 1
  • “Intro” pacing — you have ~2s before a skimmer scrolls past

Verified vs assumption

  • VERIFIEDMuted hero loops: first frame must identify context unaided (GogoScreen, demovexa, cutmap corpus)
  • VERIFIEDGenre opens inside product or one-line question — measured beat-01 data, not opinion
  • VERIFIEDCase-study recordings work as evidence: “states, not tours” (viewport guide, datamorph practice)
  • ASSUMEDViewer = hiring skim, ~2s of patience → drives evidence-first ordering. If wrong, the ladder still holds — it’s also how a buyer judges a store.
  • ASSUMEDEach beat can deep-link via URL (filter state in URL) — still unverified from the skill.
